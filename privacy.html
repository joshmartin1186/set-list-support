Privacy Policy

Set List · Last updated October 28, 2026

Set List does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. This policy explains what that means.

What the app stores

The songs, chord charts, lyrics, categories and set lists you enter are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. It makes no network requests.

Files you import

When you add song files from your computer or the Files app, the app reads them on your device to create songs. The files are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
